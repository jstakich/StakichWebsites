Getting started

What happens after I pay?

Stripe emails you a receipt. I build your website from your order form answers, your photos, and your Facebook and Google pages. In about 1–2 weeks I text or email you a link to see it. You ask for any changes, and once you're happy, it goes live.

Do I need to send you anything else?

No. Your order form has what I need. If you think of more photos or details later, text or email them anytime.

How long until my website is live?

Usually 1–2 weeks after you order. Changes you ask for during the review are part of that.

Can I see my website before it goes live?

Yes. I send you a private link first. Nothing goes live until you say it looks good.

I have an old website. What do I do with it?

Keep it running until your new website is live. After that, you can cancel your old website plan (like GoDaddy Websites + Marketing, Wix, or Squarespace). Keep paying for your domain, though. That's your web address.

Can you use the words and photos from my old website?

Yes. I move over anything worth keeping, so nothing important gets lost.

I don't have a logo or many photos. Is that OK?

Yes. Send what you have, even photos from your phone. I can also use photos from your Facebook page.

Your domain (web address)

I picked a new domain on the form. What do I do?

Nothing. I buy it, set it up, and renew it every year. It's included in your plan.

I already own my domain. What do I do?

This lets me point your domain to your new website. It takes about 2 minutes, and you never share your password. Your email keeps working, and the domain stays yours.

GoDaddy:

  1. Sign in to GoDaddy and tap your name at the top right, then Account Settings, then Delegate Access.
  2. Tap Invite to Access, type Jeremy Stakich and Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com.
  3. Choose Products & Domains, then tap Invite.

Squarespace: Sign in, open Domains, tap your domain, then Permissions. Invite Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com and allow it to manage the domain.

Wix: Sign in, open your site's Settings, then Roles & Permissions. Tap Invite People, type Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com, and choose Admin (Co-Owner).

Any other company: Sign in and look for Delegate Access, Share Access, or Permissions, and invite Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com.

Not sure where you bought it? Search your email for your domain name and the word "renewal." The company that sends the renewal emails is the one.

Please don't cancel or move your domain. Once your new website is live, you can cancel your old website plan, but keep paying the yearly domain renewal.

Will my business email stop working?

No. If you get email at your domain (like you@yourbusiness.com), I leave the email settings alone, so it keeps working the whole time.

Can I get an email address like you@yourbusiness.com?

Email isn't part of the website plans, and I don't set it up. The cheap way to get you@yourbusiness.com in Gmail is Google Workspace Business Starter. It's about $7 a month per person if you pay for a year, or about $8 a month if you pay month to month. You buy it from Google and pay Google. It works in the Gmail app like a normal Gmail account, with your business address on it.

Microsoft 365 Business Basic is about the same price if you'd rather use Outlook. Google's prices can change, so check their site before you buy.

What happens to my domain if I cancel?

If you already owned it, it stays yours, just like before. If I bought it for you and your account is paid up, I'll transfer it to you when you ask. You can take it anywhere.

Changes and updates

How do I ask for a change?

Text (423) 227-7681 or email Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com. Tell me what to change and on which page, and attach any new photos. That's it.

What's a small update?

Things like new hours, prices, phone number, photos, a new service, or changing some text. Starter and Growth include 2 small updates each month. Pro gets priority same-day updates when possible.

How fast are changes made?

Most small changes are done in 1–2 business days. If your website is ever down or broken, I fix it the same day.

Can I edit the website myself?

You don't need to. There's no login to learn or forget. Just text or email what you want changed and I'll do it.

Can I add more pages?

Starter includes up to 5 pages, Growth up to 8, and Pro up to 12. Blog posts and your privacy policy page don't count toward that. Need more? Ask about moving up a plan.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Text or email me which plan you want. I'll tell you exactly what changes before anything is charged.

Who writes my blog posts?

I do, on Growth (1 post a month) and Pro (2 posts a month). Each post is ready to share on Facebook or other social media. Got a topic or a question customers ask a lot? Send it and I'll write about it.

Do you post on my Facebook for me?

No. I write the blog posts on Growth and Pro. You share them yourself with the buttons on your website. It only takes a few seconds.

Google and getting found

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. Every plan is built to show up in Google search, with the right titles, descriptions, and local info. Google usually takes a few weeks to show a new website, so don't worry if it's not there on day one. No one can honestly promise the #1 spot, but a fast, clear website helps a lot.

What does the Pro plan do with Google and Bing?

I manage and update your Google and Bing listings. From the start, add Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com as Manager. If you don't have a Google or Bing listing yet, you'll need to set that up. The steps are on your thank-you page, Get Found on Google, and Get Found on Bing.

How do I get more Google reviews?

Ask happy customers right after a good job, and text them your review link. Never pay or give discounts for reviews, because that's against Google's rules. On Growth and Pro, your website shows your Google reviews.

Can I see how many people visit my website?

I don't put tracking on your website, to protect your customers' privacy. The best signs are more calls, texts, and quote requests. Ask new customers "How did you find us?" If you have a Google Business Profile, Google also shows you how many people found you there.

Calls, texts, and quote requests

Where do quote requests and messages go?

Straight to your email, so they pop up on your phone right away. Calls and texts from your website go to your phone number.

I got an email asking me to confirm my form. Is that real?

Yes. The first time your quote form is used, you may get one email from FormSubmit asking you to confirm. Tap the button once, so every request reaches you.

I'm not getting quote request emails.

Check your spam or junk folder and mark the email as "not spam." Still nothing? Text me at (423) 227-7681 and I'll test it right away.

My phone number, hours, or address changed.

Text or email me the new info. It counts as a small update, and I'll also update your Google listing if you're on Pro.

Billing and canceling

When am I charged?

You pay the setup fee and your first month together. After that, your monthly plan is charged automatically on the same date each month.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

All major cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, processed securely by Stripe. I never see or store your full card number.

How do I update my card or get a receipt?

Stripe emails a receipt every month. To change your card, open that email and use Stripe's update-card link. I never see your card number, so don't email it to me. If you can't find the receipt, email Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com and I'll send you Stripe's page. Same if you have a question about a charge.

Is there a contract?

No. You can cancel anytime.

How do I cancel?

Email Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com. Your website stays online until the end of your billing month, and you won't be charged again after that.

Can I get a refund?

Full refund if you cancel before design work starts. Half of the setup fee back if you cancel after work starts but before your website goes live. No refund after it's live, since the work is done. Details: Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Does my monthly price stay the same?

Yes. You keep paying the monthly price of the plan you picked. It doesn't jump after the first year.

What you own

What's mine?

Your logo, photos, and words are always yours. If you already owned your domain, it stays yours. If I bought it for you, I'll transfer it to you if you ever leave.

What happens to my website if I cancel?

It goes offline at the end of your billing month. The website design and code belong to Stakich Websites. Full details are in the Terms of Service.

Is my website safe and backed up?

Yes. Every plan includes HTTPS (the padlock), security, monitoring, and backups, so any change can be undone.

Do you sell my information or track my visitors?

No. I never sell your information, and I don't track visitors on your website. See the Privacy Policy.

Still have a question?

Call, text, or email me. I usually reply the same day.
