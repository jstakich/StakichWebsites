Get on Google & Bing

Put your business on Google Maps, Google Search, and Bing in about 15 minutes.

This takes about 15 minutes. Google and Bing require the business owner to do these steps, so I can't do them for you. Once they're done, I handle everything else: your hours, photos, services, and website link.

Step 1: Get your business on Google

This puts your business on Google Search and Google Maps.

  1. Go to google.com/business and tap Manage now.
  2. Sign in with your Google account (your Gmail). No account? Tap Create account. It takes 2 minutes.
  3. Type your business name. If it shows up, pick it. If not, choose Add your business to Google.
  4. Choose the category that fits best, like "Lawn care service" or "Barber shop."
  5. If customers come to you, add your address. If you go to customers, add the towns you serve instead.
  6. Add your business phone number and your website address. No website yet? Skip it. I'll add it later.
  7. Verify. Google will ask you to confirm by text, phone call, email, or a short video of your business. Follow what it asks. A video can take a few days to be approved.
  8. Skip the rest of the extra questions. I'll fill them in for you.

Step 2: Add me as a manager on Google

This lets me update your listing for you. You stay the owner and can remove me anytime.

  1. While signed in, search Google for your business name. Your Business Profile appears at the top.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮), then Business Profile settings.
  3. Tap People and access, then Add.
  4. Enter Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com, choose Manager, and tap Invite.

Step 3: Get your business on Bing

Bing powers search on Windows computers, Microsoft Edge, and other search engines. You can copy your Google listing in a few clicks.

  1. Go to bingplaces.com and sign in with the same Google account from Step 1.
  2. Choose Import from Google Business Profile and allow access.
  3. Select your business and tap Import. That's it. Bing copies everything over, and your listing usually goes live within a few days.
  4. Optional: open Manage users, add Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com, and I can update Bing for you too.

Step 4: Tell me you're done

Check off what you finished and tap the button. I'll get an email and take it from there.
