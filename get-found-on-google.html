Why your Google listing matters

When someone nearby searches for what you do, like "lawn care near me," Google shows a map with a few local businesses above the regular websites. Each one is a Google Business Profile: your name, stars and reviews, hours, photos, and a big Call button.

For most local businesses, this listing brings in more calls than the website itself. Google's listing is free, but managing it the right way and keeping it fresh takes time every month. That's what the Pro plan covers.

What I do for you in the Pro plan

  • Listing finished: the right categories, your services and prices, hours, service area, and a description written to match what customers search for.
  • Photos: your logo, a cover photo, and photos of your work.
  • Connected to your website: your website, phone, and quote or booking link all go to the right place.
  • More reviews: a "Review us on Google" button on your website and a review link you can text to happy customers.
  • A monthly Google post, like a special offer or seasonal reminder, so your listing stays active.
  • Always up to date: new hours, holiday hours, photos, and services. Just text me the change.

Your part: about 10 minutes

From the start, add me as Manager. If you don't have a Google listing yet, that part is yours. Google only lets the business owner add one. You never share your password, and you stay the owner.

Step 1: If you don't have a Google listing yet

  1. Go to google.com/business and tap Manage now.
  2. Sign in with your Google account (your Gmail). No account? Tap Create account. It takes 2 minutes.
  3. Type your business name. If it shows up, pick it. If not, choose Add your business to Google.
  4. Choose the category that fits best, like "Lawn care service" or "Barber shop."
  5. If customers come to you, add your address. If you go to customers, add the towns you serve instead.
  6. Add your business phone number. Skip the website and extra questions. I'll fill those in.
  7. Verify. Google will ask you to confirm by text, phone call, email, or a short video of your business. Follow what it asks. A video can take a few days to be approved.

Step 2: Add me as a manager

This lets me do everything above from my own account. You can remove me anytime with one tap.

  1. While signed in, search Google for your business name. Your Business Profile appears at the top.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮), then Business Profile settings.
  3. Tap People and access, then Add.
  4. Enter Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com, choose Manager, and tap Invite.

What happens next

As soon as you tap the button below, I accept your invite and update your hours, photos, and info, usually within 2–3 business days. A brand-new listing can take a few weeks to start showing up high in Google Maps. Reviews and monthly posts help it climb.
