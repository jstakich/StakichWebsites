How Much Does a Website Cost for a Small Business? (2026 Prices)

A small business website usually costs $500 to $3,000 to build with a freelancer or small studio, plus $30 to $150 per month for hosting, security, and updates. DIY options like GoDaddy, Bluehost, and Wix look cheaper, but you build the site yourself and pay yearly add-on fees. Agencies often charge $5,000 or more.
