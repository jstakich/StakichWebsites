About us

Honest legal help from neighbors who listen.

Our story

I'm Sarah Harper. I grew up in Bradley County, and I've practiced law here for more than 20 years. I started this firm because I saw too many people afraid to call a lawyer, worried about the cost or being talked down to.

My partner, David Lowe, joined me in 2012. David focuses on personal injury and business law, and I focus on family law, estates, and probate. Between us we have more than 30 years of experience in East Tennessee courts.

When you call, you'll talk to a real person, and you'll always meet with an attorney. We explain your options in plain English, give you clear fees up front, and fight hard for you.

Our promise to you

  • You always meet with an attorney
  • Clear flat fees for common paperwork
  • No fee unless we win injury cases
