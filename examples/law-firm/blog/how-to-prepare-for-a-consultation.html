How to Prepare for Your First Meeting With a Lawyer

By Sarah Harper · September 7, 2026

Meeting a lawyer for the first time can feel nerve-racking. It doesn't have to be. A first meeting is just a conversation about what happened and what your options are.

What to bring

  • Any papers about your situation, like letters, contracts, or court papers
  • Photos, if they help explain what happened
  • A short timeline of important dates
  • Names of other people involved

Questions to ask

  • What are my options?
  • How long will this take?
  • What will it cost, and how do you bill?
  • Who will I work with day to day?

Be open and honest

What you share in a consultation is confidential. Tell the whole story, even the parts you're not proud of. It helps your lawyer give you real advice.
