Our Work

A few recent jobs around Cleveland and Bradley County. New photos are added every month.

Crisp edges along the sidewalk

Every visit, we edge the sidewalk and driveway with a string trimmer. That clean line is what makes a yard look finished.

Hedges trimmed neat and even

We shape hedges and shrubs with sharp trimmers so they grow back thick and healthy, then clean up every clipping.

Fall leaves cleared out

Leaves pile up fast in the fall. We blow them out of the lawn, beds, and corners, then haul them away or mulch them in.
