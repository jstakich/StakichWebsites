Green Leaf Lawn Care

Questions & answers

Quick answers about scheduling, prices, and how we care for your yard.

Scheduling

Do I need to be home when you come?

No. Just leave the gate unlocked and let us know about any pets. We text you when we arrive and when we're done.

What if it rains on my service day?

We'll text you and come the next dry day. Mowing wet grass leaves ruts and clumps, so we wait.

How soon can you start?

Most new yards start the same week. Book online or call, and we'll text you your first day.

Pricing

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We'll look at your yard and give you a clear price, usually within 48 hours. You can also use our online price estimate.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. Weekly service is month-to-month, and you can pause or stop anytime.

How do I pay?

We send a simple bill by text or email once a month. You can pay by card, bank transfer, or check.

Lawn Care

How short do you cut the grass?

Most Tennessee lawns are fescue, so we cut at about 3.5 inches. Taller grass shades out weeds and holds water better in summer.

When is the best time to aerate and seed?

For fescue lawns, early fall is best, from about September to mid-October. Cooler nights and warm soil help new seed grow.

Do you bag the clippings?

Usually no. Small clippings break down and feed your lawn. We bag them if the grass is very long or if you ask.

Do you work on business properties?

Yes. We care for offices, churches, rentals, and HOA common areas. Call or text for a custom quote.
