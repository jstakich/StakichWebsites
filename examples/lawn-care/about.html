Green Leaf Lawn Care

About us

It started with one push mower. Now it's a crew you can count on.

Our story

I'm Caleb. When I was 15, I mowed my neighbor's yard with my dad's push mower. Then the house next door asked, and the one after that. By the end of that summer I had 20 yards and a lot of sore muscles.

I never stopped. Today Green Leaf Lawn Care has a full crew, good equipment, and hundreds of yards across Bradley County. But the idea is still the same: show up on time, do it right, and treat every yard like it's our own.

We come on the same day each week, text you when we're on the way, and never leave without blowing off your driveway and walks. No contracts. If you're not happy, you can stop anytime.

Our promise to you

  • Same crew, same day every week
  • Licensed, insured, and background-checked
  • Text updates when we arrive
  • Driveway and walks blown off every visit
  • No contracts, cancel anytime
