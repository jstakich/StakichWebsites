Move-Out Cleaning Checklist: How to Get Your Deposit Back

By Tasha Greene · September 7, 2026

Most deposit charges come from the same few places: the kitchen, the bathroom, and the floors. Clean these well and you have a much better chance of getting every dollar back.

Kitchen

  • Inside and outside of the fridge, including the drawers.
  • Inside the oven and under the burners.
  • Inside every cabinet and drawer.
  • Counters, backsplash, and sink.
  • The front of the dishwasher and microwave.

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, including the base and behind it.
  • Tub, shower, and tile grout.
  • Mirror, sink, and cabinet inside and out.
  • Exhaust fan cover.

Every room

  • Baseboards and door frames.
  • Light switches and outlet covers.
  • Window sills and blinds.
  • Closet shelves.
  • Vacuum and mop every floor last.

Take photos when you're done

Walk through with your phone and take photos of every room. If there's ever a question about your deposit, you'll have proof.

Short on time?

Moving is a lot. Our move-out clean covers every item on this list, inside cabinets, the fridge, and the oven included.
