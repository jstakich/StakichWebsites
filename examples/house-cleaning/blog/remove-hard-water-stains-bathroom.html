How to Get Rid of Hard Water Stains in Your Bathroom

By Tasha Greene · September 21, 2026

Much of East Tennessee has hard water, which means it carries a lot of minerals. When the water dries, those minerals leave white, chalky spots on faucets, glass, and tile.

What you need

  • White vinegar
  • A spray bottle
  • Baking soda
  • A soft scrub brush or old toothbrush
  • A microfiber cloth

Step 1: Spray with vinegar

Mix equal parts white vinegar and warm water in a spray bottle. Spray the spots until they're wet and let it sit for 10 to 15 minutes.

Step 2: Scrub tough spots

For thick buildup, make a paste of baking soda and a little water. Scrub gently with a soft brush. A toothbrush works great around faucet bases.

Step 3: Rinse and dry

Rinse with clean water and dry right away with a microfiber cloth. Drying is the secret to a streak-free shine.

Don't use vinegar on stone

Vinegar can damage marble, granite, and other natural stone. Use a cleaner made for stone on those surfaces instead.

Keep spots from coming back

Keep a squeegee in the shower and use it on the glass after each shower. It takes 20 seconds and stops most spots before they start.
