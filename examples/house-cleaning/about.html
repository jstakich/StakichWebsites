About us

It started with one bucket and a lot of trust.

Our story

I'm Tasha. I started cleaning homes on my own in 2014, with one bucket of supplies and a used car. My clients trusted me with their keys, their pets, and their homes, and I never took that lightly.

As word spread, I needed help. I hired people I'd trust in my own house, trained them on my checklist, and made sure they were background-checked and insured. That's how Sparkle & Shine became a team.

We still work the way I did on day one. You get the same cleaner every visit when we can, we bring everything, and if anything isn't right, we come back within 24 hours and fix it for free.

Our promise to you

  • Background-checked, insured cleaners
  • Same cleaner every visit when possible
  • We bring all supplies
  • Eco-friendly products on request
  • 24-hour re-clean promise
