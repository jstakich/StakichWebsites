Our story

My dad started Guardian Pest Control with one truck and a promise: treat every home like it's your own. I took over in 2016, and we still keep that promise every day.

Our technicians are trained, licensed, and background-checked. We use the least product needed to get the job done, and we always explain what we're using and why.
