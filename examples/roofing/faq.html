Questions & answers

Quick answers about inspections, insurance, prices, and what to expect.

Inspections

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. We check your roof, take photos, and explain what we found. There's no cost and no pressure.

Do I need to be home for the inspection?

It helps, so we can show you what we found, but it's not required. We'll text you the photos either way.

Insurance

Will insurance pay for my roof?

If you have storm damage, often yes. We inspect for free and help with your claim.

Will you meet with my insurance adjuster?

Yes. We meet the adjuster on your roof and make sure every damaged spot is documented.

Pricing

Is the online estimate my final price?

No. It's a typical range for our area. After a free inspection, you get an exact, written price with materials and timing.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, with low monthly payment options for roofs, gutters, and siding.

Are there any deals right now?

Yes. Mention this website when you schedule your free inspection and save $500 on a full roof replacement.

The Job

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are done in 1–2 days. Bigger or steeper roofs may take a little longer.

Do you clean up after?

Yes. We tarp your landscaping, haul away all the old roofing, and run a magnet over the yard and driveway to pick up nails.

What warranty do you offer?

Our work comes with a workmanship warranty, and we offer manufacturer-backed warranties up to a lifetime on shingles.
