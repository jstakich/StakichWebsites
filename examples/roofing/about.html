About us

25 years of roofs done right. Still family-owned.

Our story

I'm Wade. I've been working on roofs for more than 25 years. I started as a young man carrying shingles up a ladder, and I've loved this trade ever since. Ironwood Roofing is a family business. My son runs one of our crews, and my wife keeps the office running.

Our own crews do every job, from tear-off to cleanup, so the quality never depends on a subcontractor. I still visit every job site, and I still climb up to check the work.

After a storm, we inspect for free and work directly with your insurance company to make the claim simple. And if you only need a small repair, I'll tell you. A roof is a big decision, and you deserve an honest answer.
