How Often Should You Get a Haircut? A Simple Guide by Style

By Marco Reyes · October 4, 2026

Hair grows about half an inch a month. That doesn't sound like much, but on a short cut it makes a big difference. The right time between cuts depends on your style.

Skin fades and very short cuts: every 2 to 3 weeks

Fades lose their sharp look fast, because even a little growth shows. If you want your fade to look fresh all the time, book every 2 weeks.

Short classic cuts: every 3 to 4 weeks

A classic scissor or clipper cut stays neat for about a month. After that, the sides start to puff out and the neckline gets fuzzy.

Medium length: every 4 to 6 weeks

Styles with more length on top can go a little longer. A trim every 4 to 6 weeks keeps the shape and gets rid of split ends.

Longer hair: every 6 to 8 weeks

If you're growing your hair out, you still need trims. Regular cleanups keep it growing in a good shape instead of just getting bigger.

Tips to stretch time between cuts

  • Book a line-up between cuts to clean the edges.
  • Use a little product to control the top.
  • Book your next cut before you leave, so you get the time you want.
