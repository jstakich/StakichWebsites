How to Grow and Shape a Beard That Looks Good

By Marco Reyes · August 23, 2026

A good beard takes patience. Most guys give up in the first month because it feels itchy or looks patchy. Stick with it, and follow these steps.

Give it 4 to 6 weeks

Let it grow without trimming for at least a month. Patchy spots often fill in once the hair gets longer.

Beat the itch

The itchy stage is normal. Wash your beard with a gentle beard wash and use a few drops of beard oil every day to soften the hair and the skin under it.

Find your neckline

The most common mistake is trimming the neckline too high. A good rule: your neckline should sit about two fingers above your Adam's apple.

Keep the cheek line natural

Clean up stray hairs on your cheeks, but don't cut the line too low. A natural line looks fuller.

Daily care

  • Brush or comb it every day.
  • Use beard oil after a shower.
  • Trim stray hairs once a week.
  • Get a beard shape-up every 2 to 4 weeks.

A barber can shape your beard to fit your face, which makes it easy to keep up at home between visits.
