Main Street Barber Co.

Questions & answers

Quick answers about booking, prices, walk-ins, and kids cuts.

Booking

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Booking online guarantees your time, but walk-ins are always welcome.

Can I pick my barber?

Yes. Choose your barber when you book, or pick 'First available' for the soonest time.

How early should I arrive?

Five minutes before your appointment is perfect.

What if I'm running late?

Text us. If you're more than 10 minutes late, we may need to move you to the next open time.

Prices

Do you accept cards?

Yes. All major cards, Apple Pay, and cash.

Do you have discounts?

Kids 12 and under have their own lower price, and seniors 65 and up save $3 on any cut. New clients save $5 on their first visit.

Can I tip on a card?

Yes. You can add a tip when you pay with a card.

Kids

Is my child too young for a haircut here?

Not at all. We cut kids of every age and we're patient with first-timers. First cuts get a certificate and a lock of hair to keep.

What's the best time to bring kids?

Weekday mornings are the quietest. Book ahead so you're not waiting with a restless little one.
