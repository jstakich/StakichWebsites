About us

A real barbershop on Main Street.

Our story

I'm Marco. I picked up my first pair of clippers at 16, cutting my brothers' hair on our back porch. After barber school and years behind someone else's chair, I opened Main Street Barber Co. in 2011 with two chairs and a lot of hope.

Fifteen years later, we have four master barbers who know your name and how you like your cut. We still finish every cut with a hot towel, and the coffee is still on us.

This shop is for everyone: dads and sons, first haircuts, wedding parties, and regulars who've sat in the same chair for years. Walk in any time, or book ahead to skip the wait.
