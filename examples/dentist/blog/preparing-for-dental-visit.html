How to Prepare for a Routine Dental Visit

By Elise Morgan · November 3, 2026

Regular visits give you time to talk with your dentist about your oral health and questions. The care plan depends on your individual needs.

Before you arrive

  • Update your medication and health history.
  • Make a note of any changes or questions you want to discuss.
  • Bring your insurance card if you plan to use coverage.
  • Call ahead if you need help with directions or accessibility.

During the visit

Ask the team to explain any recommended exam, imaging, or follow-up care. You can discuss your concerns and ask about the expected cost before making a decision.

After the visit

Follow the instructions your dental team provides and contact the office if you need clarification. For personal advice, speak with your dentist.

Further reading: American Dental Association: Seeing the Dentist
