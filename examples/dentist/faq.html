Northshore Family Dentistry

Questions & answers

Answers about scheduling, first visits, fees, and patient privacy.

Appointments

How do I request an appointment?

Use the online request form or call the office. A team member will confirm the date and time.

First visit

What should I bring?

Bring a medication list, insurance information if applicable, and any dental records you would like the dentist to review.

Will I receive treatment at my first appointment?

That depends on your needs and the appointment. The dentist will explain findings and discuss recommendations with you.

Fees & coverage

Do you accept my dental insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. Contact the office with your insurer and plan details so the team can discuss next steps.

Privacy

Can I send health details through this form?

Please use the form only for scheduling. Call the practice directly to discuss private health information.

Urgent concerns

What if I have severe or urgent symptoms?

Call the office for guidance. For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
