Helpful answers

Questions new patients often ask

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, we welcome new patients. Call or use the appointment request to ask about current availability.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance information if applicable, and a list of medications or questions for your dentist.

Can you tell me what my insurance will cover?

Our team can help review your plan and provide an estimate. Final benefits are determined by your insurer.

What if I need urgent dental care?

Call the office and describe the situation so the team can explain available appointment options and next steps.
