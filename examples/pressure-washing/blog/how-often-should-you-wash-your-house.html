How Often Should You Wash Your House?

By Kevin Tran · September 21, 2026

Chattanooga's warm, humid weather is great for growing things, and that includes mold, mildew, and algae on your siding. That green or black film is more than ugly. Over time, it can stain siding and wear down paint.

The simple answer

Most homes in our area should be washed once a year. If your home is surrounded by trees or has a shady north side, you may see green come back sooner.

Signs it's time

  • Green or black streaks on the shady side
  • Cobwebs and dirt around windows and eaves
  • Siding that looks dull or chalky
  • You're getting ready to sell or have guests coming

Soft-wash vs. pressure wash

High pressure can crack vinyl, strip paint, and force water behind your siding. Soft-washing uses low pressure, about like a garden hose, plus a cleaning solution that kills mildew at the root. It cleans better and lasts longer.

Best time of year

Spring and fall are the most popular times. Spring washes off winter grime and pollen. Fall gets your home clean before the holidays. Any dry day above freezing works.
