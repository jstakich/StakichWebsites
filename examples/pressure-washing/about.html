About us

Careful work, honest prices, and a walk-around on every job.

Our story

I'm Kevin. I grew up in Chattanooga, and I've always loved seeing something go from dirty to like-new. I started Blue Ridge Pressure Washing to give homeowners an honest, careful option.

Too many people have had siding, shingles, or decks damaged by someone using too much pressure. We use the right pressure and the right cleaning solution for every surface, so your home gets cleaned, not hurt.

Every job ends with a walk-around. You see the results before we leave, and if anything isn't right, we fix it on the spot. We're fully insured for $2 million, and we treat your home like it's our own.

Our promise to you

  • Soft-wash safe for siding and roofs
  • $2 million insured
  • Eco-friendly cleaning solutions
  • Plants rinsed and protected
  • Satisfaction walk-around on every job
