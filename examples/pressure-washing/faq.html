Blue Ridge Pressure Washing

Questions & answers

Quick answers about booking, prices, and how we keep your home safe.

Booking

Do I need to be home?

No. We just need access to an outside water spigot. We text you before we start and send photos when we're done.

What if it rains on my day?

Light rain is usually fine, but we'll move you to the next dry day if storms roll in. We'll text you first.

How long does a job take?

Most house washes take 2 to 3 hours. A driveway takes about an hour.

Pricing

Is the online estimate my final price?

It's a close range for most homes. After you send a photo or we take a quick look, we give you a firm price before any work starts.

Do you offer any deals?

Yes. Book a house wash and driveway cleaning together and save 15%.

Safety

Is pressure washing safe for my siding?

Yes. We use low-pressure soft-washing on siding and roofs, so nothing gets damaged.

Will the cleaning solution hurt my plants or pets?

We use eco-friendly solutions, and we wet and rinse your plants before and after. Keep pets inside while we work and until things dry.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $2 million in insurance for your peace of mind.

Results

How long does it last?

Most homes stay clean for 1–2 years depending on shade and weather. Roofs often stay streak-free for 3 years or more.

Can you get oil stains out of concrete?

We can lighten most oil stains a lot with hot water and a degreaser. Very old stains may not come out all the way, and we'll tell you up front.

No matches. Text us your question instead.

Ready when you are.

Call, text, or book a wash online. We usually reply the same day.
