Rosewood Hills Health & Rehabilitation

Take a Look Inside

Families want to see where their loved one will live. Here's a look inside Rosewood Hills.

Therapy gym and rehab

Our physical therapists help residents rebuild strength and balance, one step at a time. Many rehab patients go home stronger than before their surgery.

Comfortable private rooms

Each room has a comfy bed, an armchair, and a big window for natural light. Families are welcome to bring photos, quilts, and favorite things from home.

Garden courtyard

On nice days, residents and staff spend time together in our sunny garden courtyard. It's a favorite spot for visits, games, and fresh air.

Ready when you are.

Call, text, or schedule a tour online. We usually reply the same day.
