7 Easy Ways to Stay Close to a Loved One in a Nursing Home

By Grace Kim · August 25, 2026

When someone you love moves into a nursing home, staying connected matters for both of you. Here are simple ideas that work well, even if you live far away.

Ideas for visits

  • Join an activity together, like music hour or bingo
  • Take a walk in the garden courtyard
  • Bring a favorite snack (check with the nurse first)
  • Look through old photos together

Ideas when you can't visit

  • Schedule a weekly video call with the activities team
  • Mail cards or kids' drawings
  • Send a short voice message they can play again

Keep visits short and sweet when needed

For people with dementia, short, calm visits at the same time each day often go better than long ones. Ask our memory care team what works best.

Stay in touch with the team

Get to know your loved one's nurse and aides. A quick call each week keeps you up to date and builds trust.
