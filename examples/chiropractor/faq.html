Evergreen Chiropractic

Questions & answers

Answers about first visits, booking, care discussions, and privacy.

Appointments

How do I request an appointment?

Use the online request form or call the office. A team member will confirm the date and time.

First visit

What should I bring?

Bring a list of medications, relevant health conditions, and questions you want to discuss.

Care options

Will I receive spinal manipulation?

Not automatically. The doctor reviews your health history and discusses options, benefits, risks, and alternatives before care.

Can I decline or stop care?

Yes. You decide whether to proceed and can ask questions or stop at any time.

Fees & coverage

Do you accept my insurance?

Participation and coverage vary. Call the office with your plan details to discuss fees and benefits.

Privacy

Can I send health details through this form?

Please use the form only for scheduling. Call the practice directly to discuss private health information.

Urgent concerns

What if I have severe or urgent symptoms?

Call your medical clinician for guidance. For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
