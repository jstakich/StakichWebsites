Helpful answers

Questions before your first appointment

What happens at the first visit?

We discuss what brings you in, review your history, and explain the evaluation. The office can tell you whether treatment may be included.

How much does an appointment cost?

Call the office for current fees. We’ll explain costs before care so you can decide how to proceed.

Do you accept my insurance?

Contact the office with your plan details. Coverage varies, and the insurer determines your individual benefits.

Can you tell me if chiropractic care is right for me?

Dr. Reed can discuss your situation and explain options during an evaluation. If another type of care may be appropriate, he can talk that through with you.
