Questions to ask before chiropractic care

Dr. Thomas Reed · Example content

A first conversation can help you understand whether a chiropractic evaluation fits your needs. The clinician should ask about your health history and explain what an examination may involve.

Bring the information that matters

Make a list of health conditions, medicines, supplements, prior injuries, and questions about the symptoms or movement concerns you want to discuss. Share this information before any hands-on care is considered.

Ask about the options

You can ask what techniques may be recommended, what they involve, what benefits and risks are known, and whether alternatives are available. You can also ask how progress will be reviewed and when another clinician may be appropriate.

Understand time and cost

Ask how many visits may be proposed, the expected cost, and what your insurance may cover. The number of visits should be explained in relation to your situation, and you can revisit the plan as you go.

Spinal manipulation can cause temporary soreness or discomfort, and rare serious complications have been reported. Discuss your health history and any concerns with a qualified clinician before deciding on care. This article is general information, not personal medical advice.
