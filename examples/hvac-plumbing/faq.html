Summit Heating, Air & Plumbing

Questions & answers

Quick answers about booking, prices, emergencies, and our work.

Booking

Do I need an account to book online?

No. Pick a service, a day, and a time, and leave your name, phone, and address. We text you to confirm.

Do you really answer 24/7?

Yes. A real person from our team answers day and night, including weekends and holidays.

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

Our average arrival time is about 60 minutes in the Cleveland area. Farther towns may take a little longer.

Pricing

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

Our emergency rate is shown upfront before any work begins. Regular booked visits are the same price any day.

Is the online estimate my final price?

It's a close range for most homes. Your technician always gives you the exact price before starting.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. New systems and water heaters can be financed with approved credit.

Our work

Which brands do you service?

All major brands, including Trane, Carrier, Lennox, Goodman, and Rheem.

What warranty do you offer?

All repairs come with a 1-year labor warranty, plus the maker's warranty on parts.

Maintenance

What comes with the maintenance plan?

Two tune-ups a year (AC in spring, heat in fall), front-of-the-line service, and 15% off repairs, all for $15 a month.

How often should I change my air filter?

Every 1 to 3 months. Check it monthly if you have pets or allergies.
