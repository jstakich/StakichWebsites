Our Work

A few sample jobs like the ones we do around Ooltewah and Hamilton County. New photos would go here each month on a live site.

Algae pulled off the rocks

This backyard pond had thick string algae on the rocks and falls. We pulled it out, rinsed the faces, and got the water moving again.

A full spring cleanout

Leaves and muck had sat all winter. We drained, scooped the bottom, cleaned the pump, and refilled. The fish went back in the same day.

Waterfall running clear again

The falls had gone green and the flow was weak. After a cleanup and a filter rinse, you could hear the water from the patio again.
