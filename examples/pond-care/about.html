Clear Water Pond Care

About us

A local guy who cleans ponds, not a big lake company.

Our story

I'm Cody. I clean backyard ponds around Ooltewah. Most of the calls I get are the same: the water went green, the rocks are slimy, and you can't see the fish.

I don't sell you a giant lake plan. I come out, look at the pond, pull the algae, clean the pump and filter, and tell you straight what it needs. If a full cleanout can wait until fall, I'll say so.

You get me on the job. I'll text you a price from a photo when I can, show up when I said I would, and leave the pond looking like water again.

Our promise to you

  • Algae removal is the main job
  • Fish are held safely on a full cleanout
  • Clear price before any work starts
  • Text updates when I'm on the way
  • No long contract
