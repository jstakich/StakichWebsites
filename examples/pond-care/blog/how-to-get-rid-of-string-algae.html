How to Get Rid of String Algae in a Backyard Pond

By Cody · October 6, 2026

String algae is the number one call I get. It hangs off the waterfall, coats the rocks, and makes a nice pond look like a swamp. You can get ahead of it without tearing the whole pond apart.

Pull it out. Don't just blast it.

A hose knocks some of it loose, then it sinks and feeds the next bloom. Twist it onto a stick or a rake and take it out of the pond. Get it off the property so it can't rot back into the water.

Clean the filter

A clogged filter is why a lot of ponds go green. Rinse the pads in a bucket of pond water, not chlorinated hose water, so you don't kill the good bacteria.

Don't overfeed the fish

Extra food turns into extra waste, and extra waste turns into algae. Feed only what they finish in a few minutes.

When to call

If the falls are a solid green mat, or you can't see the bottom, a visit is faster than fighting it every weekend. Book online or text a photo.
