About us

A local handyman who shows up and does it right.

Our story

I'm Mike. I started Hometown Handyman after 15 years of fixing things for friends, neighbors, and my church family in Bradley County.

I know how hard it is to find someone who calls back, shows up on time, and charges a fair price. That's the whole idea behind this business.

When you call, you get me. I'll give you a clear price before I start, clean up when I'm done, and stand behind my work for a full year.

Our promise to you

  • Licensed and insured
  • Clear price before any work starts
  • On time, or I call ahead
  • Clean-up included on every job
  • 1-year guarantee on my work
