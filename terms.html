Terms of Service

The simple terms for working with Stakich Websites.

1. Who we are

Stakich Websites ("we," "us") is owned and operated by Jeremy Stakich, a sole proprietor based in Charleston, Tennessee, USA. We design, build, host, and maintain websites for small businesses. Contact: Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com.

2. Services and plans

We offer three plans, each with a one-time setup fee and a monthly care plan:

  • Starter: $499 setup, then $49 per month. Up to 5 pages, hosting, HTTPS, Google search setup, and small monthly updates.
  • Growth: $899 setup, then $79 per month. Everything in Starter, up to 8 pages, one blog post per month, social media captions, and a reviews section.
  • Pro: $1,499 setup, then $129 per month. Everything in Growth, up to 12 pages, booking/menu/quote tools, two blog posts per month, and a monthly website visitor report.

Work outside your plan (for example, extra pages or custom features) will be quoted in writing before it starts.

3. Payment

We accept all major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, as well as Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payments are processed securely by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number. The setup fee is due before design work begins. The monthly plan begins when your website goes live and is billed automatically each month to your card on file until canceled. Prices are in US dollars.

You agree to provide accurate business information, and you confirm that you have the right to use any logos, photos, and text you send us. You are responsible for the claims your business makes on its website.

5. Ownership

Your domain name is registered in your name and belongs to you. Your logos, photos, and written content belong to you. Once all setup fees are paid, you own the design of your website. If you cancel, we will provide a copy of your website files on request.

6. Cancellation

There is no long-term contract. You may cancel your monthly plan at any time by email. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing month, and your website stays online until then. Refunds are covered by our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. Website availability

We work to keep your website online and secure, but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted service, since hosting depends on third-party providers. We do not guarantee specific Google rankings, traffic, or sales, because search engines control their own results.

8. Limitation of liability
