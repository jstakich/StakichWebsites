Why Bing matters

Bing is the search engine built into Windows computers and the Microsoft Edge browser. Bing's results also power searches on Yahoo and DuckDuckGo. Many people, especially on work computers, search with Bing without even knowing it.

A Bing Places listing puts your business on Bing Maps with your hours, phone number, photos, and website, so those customers find you instead of a competitor. Fewer businesses bother with Bing, which makes it easier to stand out.

What I do for you in the Pro plan

  • Listing checked and finished: I make sure your name, phone, address, hours, and categories match Google exactly. Matching info helps you rank higher.
  • Photos and services added, the same as your Google listing.
  • Your website added to Bing: I set up Bing Webmaster Tools so your web pages show up in Bing search, not just your map listing.
  • New pages sent to Bing automatically, so new services and blog posts show up fast.
  • Kept in sync: when your hours or services change, I update Bing too.
  • Results you can see: Bing searches and clicks in your monthly report.

Your part: about 3 minutes

Like Google, Bing only lets the business owner claim a listing. You never share your password with me.

  1. Finish Get Found on Google first. Bing copies your Google listing, so you won't type anything twice.
  2. Go to bingplaces.com and sign in with the same Google account.
  3. Choose Import from Google Business Profile and allow access.
  4. Select your business and tap Import. Bing copies everything over, and your listing usually goes live within a few days.
  5. Open Manage users, add Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com, and save. This lets me keep Bing up to date for you.
