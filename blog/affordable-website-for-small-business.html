Affordable Website for Small Business: How to Get a Pro Site Without Overpaying

By Jeremy Stakich · October 6, 2026 · PricingSmall business

Quick answer: An affordable website for a small business should cost about $500–$1,500 upfront and $30–$100 per month, and still include a mobile-friendly design, HTTPS security, Google setup, and someone to make updates. Avoid deals that don't let you own your domain or lock you into long contracts.

Cheap vs. affordable: what's the difference?

A cheap website saves money today but looks dated, loads slowly, or disappears if you stop paying a rental fee. An affordable website is fairly priced and still does its job: bringing in calls.

What a low-cost website for small business should still include

  • Mobile-friendly design (most customers are on phones)
  • HTTPS security (the padlock in the browser)
  • Your own domain name, registered in your name
  • Click-to-call buttons and a contact form
  • Basic Google setup: titles, descriptions, and local info
  • A real person to make changes when you need them

4 ways to keep your website affordable

  1. Start small. A great 3–5 page site beats a messy 20-page one.
  2. Gather your info first. Logo, photos, services, and prices ready to go saves design time.
  3. Skip features you won't use. Online stores and booking systems only when you need them.
  4. Choose month-to-month. No contracts means you're never stuck paying for poor service.

Watch out for these "cheap website" traps

  • Rental sites that vanish the day you cancel.
  • Hidden fees for every small update.
  • Templates that look exactly like your competitor's.
  • No support: you're left alone when something breaks.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest way to get a business website?

A DIY builder is cheapest upfront, but your time has value. A small studio can be the best value once you count the hours saved.

Are free websites good for small businesses?

Free sites usually show ads and a branded address, which can make your business look less professional.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Start with a small site and add pages, a blog, or booking as you grow.
