How to Get My Business on Google Maps for Free (Step by Step)

By Jeremy Stakich · October 8, 2026 · GoogleLocal search

Quick answer: To get your business on Google Maps for free, create a Google Business Profile at google.com/business, add your business name, category, and service area, then verify it (usually by video or postcard). Once verified, your business can appear on Google Maps and in "near me" searches.

Step 1: Go to Google Business Profile

Visit google.com/business and sign in with a Google account you'll keep long-term. Click Manage now.

Step 2: Add your business name and category

Use your real business name, exactly as it appears on your sign or truck. Pick the most specific category, like "Lawn care service" instead of just "Landscaper." Your category is one of the biggest factors in which searches you show up for.

Step 3: Choose a location or a service area

  • If customers visit you (shop, salon, restaurant), add your street address.
  • If you go to customers (lawn care, plumbing, cleaning), choose "I deliver goods and services to my customers," hide your address, and list the towns you serve.

That's how to get your business on Google Maps without an address showing publicly.

Step 4: Add your phone number and website

Add a phone number customers can call and your website address. Businesses with a website get more clicks and look more trustworthy. If you don't have one yet, see example websites.

Step 5: Verify your business

Google needs to confirm you're real. Most businesses verify by recording a short video showing your business, tools, or signage, or by a postcard with a code. Verification can take a few days.

Step 6: Finish your profile so you rank higher

  • Add 10 or more real photos of your work, team, and vehicles.
  • Add your hours and every service you offer.
  • Ask happy customers for Google reviews, and reply to every one.
  • Post an update every week or two (specials, recent jobs, tips).

Why a website helps your Google Maps ranking

Google trusts businesses more when the profile links to a real website with the same name, phone number, and services. A simple, fast website backs up your Maps listing and gives customers somewhere to learn more before calling. Frequently asked questions

Is Google Business Profile really free?

Yes. Creating and keeping a Google Business Profile is completely free.

Can I be on Google Maps without a storefront?

Yes. Choose a service area and hide your home address.

How long until my business shows on Google Maps?

Usually a few days after verification, sometimes up to a couple of weeks.
