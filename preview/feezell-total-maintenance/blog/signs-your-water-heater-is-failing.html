Signs Your Water Heater Is on Its Way Out

By Feezell Total Maintenance · September 24, 2026

Water heaters fail slowly, then all at once.

Signs

  • Rusty water
  • Popping or banging
  • Water on the floor around the tank
  • Not enough hot water

Repair or replace

If it is 10 to 12 years old, replacement is usually the honest answer. Call (423) 745-9053.
