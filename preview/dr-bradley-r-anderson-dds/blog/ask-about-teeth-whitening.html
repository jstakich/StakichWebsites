What to ask before teeth whitening

November 25, 2026

A conversation with the dentist can help you decide which questions matter for your smile.

Talk about your goals

Explain the change you are hoping to see and ask what is realistic with your current restorations and dental history.

Compare your options

Ask about timing, costs, and follow-up. Give yourself time to consider the answers before choosing.
