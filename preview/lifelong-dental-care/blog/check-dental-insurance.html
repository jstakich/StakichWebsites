What to ask about dental insurance

November 25, 2026

A quick call about coverage can help you plan ahead.

Confirm your plan

Have your plan name ready when you call. Ask how the office handles claims and what information it needs from you.

Discuss expected costs

Ask for an estimate of your share of the cost before scheduling additional work.
