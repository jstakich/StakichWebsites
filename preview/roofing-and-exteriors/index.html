Roofing and Exteriors is a family-owned roofing company based in Cleveland, Tennessee. Over 90% of our business comes from customer reviews and referrals.

We're big on ventilation. Most shingle makers won't back their warranty if the attic isn't vented right, so every inspection includes the best ventilation option for your home. We also replace flashing and pipe boots, and we recycle all roofing debris.
