FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to build my website?

Most websites go live in about 7 days after I receive your logo, photos, and business details. Larger Pro sites can take up to two weeks.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, security (HTTPS), backups, monitoring, and small updates like new hours, prices, photos, or services. Growth and Pro plans also include blog posts and social media captions written for you.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. Monthly plans renew each month and you can cancel anytime. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing month.

Do I own my domain name?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and the words and photos you provide are yours. If you ever leave, I'll help you move them.

I already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes. I can rebuild an outdated site, keep your current domain, and move your content over so customers can still find you.

Do you work with businesses outside Tennessee?

Yes. I work with businesses anywhere in the United States. Everything can be done by phone, email, and video call.

How do I pay?

Payments are handled securely by Stripe. You can pay by credit or debit card, and the monthly plan is billed automatically each month with an emailed receipt.
