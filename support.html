Support

Customer Support

Website changes, billing, and questions. Real help from a real person.

Email support

Email Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com anytime. I reply within one business day, usually much sooner.

Request a website change

Need new hours, prices, photos, or a new service? Email what you'd like changed (attach photos if you have them). Small updates are included in every plan.

Billing help

Update your card, get a receipt, or ask about a charge by emailing Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com. Payments are handled securely by Stripe.

How to request a change

  1. Email Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com with the subject "Website change" and your business name.
  2. Describe what you'd like changed and on which page.
  3. Attach any new photos, logos, or documents.

Most small changes are done within 1–2 business days. Pro plan customers get priority same-day updates.

Response times

  • Website down or broken: same day
  • Small updates: 1–2 business days
  • Questions and quotes: within 1 business day

Payments we accept

We accept all major credit and debit cards, plus Apple Pay and Google Pay. Every payment is processed securely through Stripe, so your card details are never stored on our website.

Canceling or pausing

There's no long contract. To cancel, email Jeremy@StakichWebsites.com. Your site stays online through the end of your billing month. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.
